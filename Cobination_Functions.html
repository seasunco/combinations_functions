Arithmetic Combinations of Functions

Just as you can add, subtract, multiply, and divide real numbers, you can perform these operations on functions. Let $f$ and $g$ be two functions with overlapping domains.

Definitions

  • Sum: $(f+g)(x) = f(x) + g(x)$
  • Difference: $(f-g)(x) = f(x) - g(x)$
  • Product: $(fg)(x) = f(x) \cdot g(x)$
  • Quotient: $\left(\frac{f}{g}\right)(x) = \frac{f(x)}{g(x)}, \quad g(x) \neq 0$

Example 1: Simplifying Arithmetic Combinations

Given $f(x) = 2x - 3$ and $g(x) = x^2 - 1$, find $(f+g)(x)$ and $(fg)(x)$.

Solution:

Sum: $(f+g)(x) = (2x - 3) + (x^2 - 1) = x^2 + 2x - 4$

Product: $(fg)(x) = (2x - 3)(x^2 - 1) = 2x^3 - 2x - 3x^2 + 3 = 2x^3 - 3x^2 - 2x + 3$

Domains of Combined Functions

The domain of an arithmetic combination of functions $f$ and $g$ consists of the intersection of their domains.

Rules for Domain

  • General Rule: Domain is $D_f \cap D_g$.
  • For Division: Exclude any $x$ where $g(x) = 0$.
  • For Square Roots: The expression inside must be $\ge 0$.

Difference Quotient

The difference quotient is fundamental in calculus. It represents the average rate of change.

$$ \frac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h}, \quad h \neq 0 $$

Composition of Functions

Composition involves applying one function to the results of another. The notation $(f \circ g)(x)$ means $f(g(x))$.

Notation

$(f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x))$

"f of g of x"

Note: Order matters! Usually $f(g(x)) \neq g(f(x))$.

Finding the Domain of a Composite Function

The domain of $f \circ g$ is the set of all $x$ in the domain of $g$ such that $g(x)$ is in the domain of $f$.

Steps:

  1. Find the domain of the inner function $g$.
  2. Find the domain of the composite function $f(g(x))$.
  3. The domain is the intersection of these two sets.
Real World

Application: Manufacturing Cost

A factory produces custom widgets. We can model the production using composite functions.

The Functions

  • $n(t)$: Number of widgets produced after $t$ hours.
    $$ n(t) = 50t $$
  • $C(n)$: Cost to produce $n$ widgets.
    $$ C(n) = 0.01n^2 + 2n + 100 $$

Problem

Find the cost as a function of time, $C(n(t))$, and calculate the cost after 4 hours.

Step 1: Substitute

Replace $n$ in $C(n)$ with $50t$.

$$ C(n(t)) = 0.01(50t)^2 + 2(50t) + 100 $$

Step 2: Simplify

$$ = 0.01(2500t^2) + 100t + 100 $$

$$ = 25t^2 + 100t + 100 $$

Step 3: Evaluate at t=4

$$ C(4) = 25(4)^2 + 100(4) + 100 $$

$$ = 25(16) + 400 + 100 $$

$$ = 400 + 400 + 100 = \$900 $$
